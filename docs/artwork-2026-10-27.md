# 2026-10-27 新增银行卡素材记录

版本：`2026102702`。对应 PR #3，3 个银行 Logo、7 张卡面。

银行 Logo 与工银卡面取自官方公开资源。Mox 与建行卡面经内置 imagegen 参考官方素材整理，属于**非官方横版示意**，不是银行发行版卡面照片，也不说明任何权益资格。生成图不用于推导产品、卡组织或活动条件；产品身份与权益仍以 PR 中的官方来源资料为准。

本轮只补素材、资源引用与说明，未修改 22 款产品的活动规则或迎新方案。所有卡面均为 1200×756 PNG；通过仓库 `catalog_asset.py` 去元数据重编码，并在 catalog.json 中记录 SHA-256。原始宣传图中的示例号码、姓名及有效期不进入公开资源。

## 生成方式与最终提示词

使用内置 `image_gen.imagegen`，未使用 CLI 或外部 API 密钥。生成图逐张检查卡组织、等级、完整边界和个人字段后，等比例近似重采样至固定画布并重编码；不通过生成图判断或修改活动资料。以下为本轮实际提示词；参考图片分别为上表对应官方原图，建行 eye+ / TRAVO 同时提供官方建行 Logo。

### eye+ Visa Signature

```text
Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: horizontal bank-card artwork for a personal wallet catalog. Reference image 1 is the official portrait CCB Asia eye+ Visa Signature card; reference image 2 is the bank's official logo. Create ONE faithful LANDSCAPE adaptation with aspect ratio 1.586:1 (1200x756 composition), viewed perfectly flat head-on, the card surface filling the ENTIRE canvas edge to edge. Preserve the dark navy-to-cyan palette, black glowing cyan eye/eclipse motif and eye+ lettering, silver chip, and correct VISA Signature identity. Reflow the portrait design into a refined horizontal composition, keep the bank logo/text accurate based on image 2, do not invent extra logos or benefits. This is a generated illustrative horizontal adaptation, not a photo of a real issued card. No frame, surrounding background, outer whitespace, shadow, reflections, phone, hands, card numbers, name, expiry, CVV, personal data, or watermark. One card only. All text upright and crisp. Do not rotate the whole portrait image; genuinely adapt the layout horizontally.
```

### TRAVO World Mastercard

```text
Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: horizontal credit-card artwork for a wallet app catalog. Reference image 1 is the official portrait CCB Asia TRAVO World Mastercard; image 2 is the official bank logo. Produce ONE faithful horizontal/landscape adaptation at 1.586:1 ratio (1200x756 composition). Perfectly flat front-on card surface filling the entire canvas edge to edge, no extra margin. Preserve the original elegant deep cobalt blue through petrol teal to nearly black textured color field, accurate bank logo upper left, widely spaced silver 'T R A V O' in the middle, silver chip/contactless mark, red-and-orange Mastercard circles lower right. Arrange these upright as a real horizontal card design; do not rotate the source wholesale. Keep identity faithful; no Visa, UnionPay, invented benefits or extra logos. No surrounding background, border, shadow, phone, hands, name, number, expiry, CVV or any personal data. No watermark. This is an illustrative adaptation of the official design, not a claim of an official issued horizontal variant.
```

### Mox

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: cleaned horizontal bank-card catalog illustration. Image 1 is an official Mox promotional photograph containing a fully visible physical Mox card in front of a phone. Extract ONLY that foreground physical card, correct its perspective into a flat front-facing landscape rectangle, and fill the entire output canvas at a 1.586:1 credit-card aspect ratio (1200x756 composition). Preserve the card's authentic turquoise/aqua gradients, white lowercase mox wordmark upper left, silver Mastercard emblem upper right, silver chip left, and dotted arrow pattern on the right. Remove only the small example cardholder name, leaving the underlying aqua background clean. No other names, card numbers, expiry, CVV or personal text. No phone, page headline, background, frame, margin, shadow, perspective or props. Keep the reference card design as faithfully as possible, not a new design. Perfectly flat card face, all details upright, fully edge-to-edge.
```

公开记录中的提示词已省去官方示例姓名字面值；实际请求只指定删除原图的同一姓名区域，不增加人物信息。

## 验证边界

- 生成的示意可能与实际发行的排版、纹理和细节不同；后续取得更好的官方横版素材时，可按稳定产品 ID 替换资源并更新哈希和内容版本。
- `catalog.json` 中相应产品说明明确标注 AI 整理；无需新增客户端字段或改变账本模型。
- 本轮发布快照没有 null 图片。即使 Schema 允许可选图片，发布含空图的后续版本仍需要确认目标客户端支持，不能推断所有旧版客户端都兼容。
